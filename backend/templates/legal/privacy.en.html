How your personal data is processed

The association you are joining is responsible for processing your personal data with great care and for not passing it on to any other party without your consent.

Your personal data is entered into eBas, the reporting system for all member associations of the national federation Sverok. When needed, we may look up your registered address via SPAR (the Swedish state population register). When you become a member of this association you also become a member of Sverok.

Sverok complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and processes your data with great care. Sverok never shares your personal data with others. Sverok may store your personal data for up to three years.

Sverok may use your personal data for membership checks by phone or text message, to verify the association's membership list. Sverok then calls or texts randomly selected people on the membership list and asks whether they are members of the association.

Sverok sends regular mailings to all members of the federation. You may also be asked to answer surveys.

The data may also be used as a basis for state grant applications, regional grant applications by the districts, the association's grant applications, and statistics.

Auditors from a government agency or region may be given access to review personal data held by Sverok's federation office, if their work requires it.
