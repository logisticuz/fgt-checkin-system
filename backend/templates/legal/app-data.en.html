How FGC Trollhättan's check-in app handles your data

When you check in at our events, the app stores your name, gamer tag, phone number, email address, which games you sign up for, your payment status and which events you have attended. The data is stored on the association's own server in Sweden.

We use it to run our tournaments, handle entry payments, keep our membership list up to date and keep long-term club statistics (for example most events attended or most games played). We do not sell your data or share it with anyone outside the association, except Sverok as described above. Tournament registrations and results are public on Start.gg, under Start.gg's own terms.

When you apply for membership, your details are first checked with Sverok's eBas system, and then a one-time code is sent to you by text message [or email] through [SMS provider]. Your personal identity number (personnummer) is only used to check and register your membership with Sverok. The app keeps it encrypted for at most 15 minutes, until your membership has been submitted, and then deletes it. We keep a record of when you accepted the bylaws and this notice, and which versions you accepted, but not your personal identity number.

We delete your phone number and email address if you have not attended any of our events for 2 years. Your gamer tag, name and event and game history are kept for club statistics. You can ask the board at any time to see, correct or delete your data: info@fgctrollhattan.se.
