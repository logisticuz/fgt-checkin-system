§1 The association

The association's name is FGC Trollhättan. The association is based in Trollhättan. The association is a non-profit association that is religiously and politically independent. The association is affiliated with Sverok. The purpose of the association is to engage in and increase interest in the gaming hobby. The association works for equal treatment, so that all members have the same opportunities and conditions to take part in and influence its activities. The association's fiscal year is 1 January to 31 December.

§2 Becoming a member

Everyone who accepts the association's bylaws has the right to become a member. A person becomes a member by registering their membership with the association in writing. The annual meeting may decide that membership is free, or that the association shall have a membership fee. If there is a membership fee, it must be paid for a person to count as a member.

Members have the right to receive information about the association, to attend and vote at the annual meeting, and to be elected to the board or the nomination committee. Members are obliged to follow the association's bylaws and rules, to keep their contact details up to date with the association, and to treat other members with respect.

§3 Ending membership

Membership is valid until the next annual meeting, but at least until the end of the fiscal year. A member who wishes to leave the association earlier shall notify the board in writing. The person is then no longer counted as a member.

A member who misbehaves can be suspended by the board. The next annual meeting must then decide whether the member shall be expelled. A suspended member has the right to attend the annual meeting and to vote on the question of their expulsion. The board and the annual meeting can revoke an expulsion or suspension.

§4 The board

The board is responsible for the association's money, activities, membership list, annual meeting and what is decided at the annual meeting. The board shall consist of at least three people, one of whom is chair. The board shall share the responsibility. The board shall decide which board members have the right to sign agreements on behalf of the association. They are called authorized signatories. The board is elected at the annual meeting and takes office as soon as the meeting ends. All members of the association can be elected to the board.

§5 Auditors

The auditor's task is to review the board's work and report on it to the next annual meeting. The association shall have one or two auditors. Board members cannot be elected auditor. The auditor does not have to be a member of the association.

§6 Nomination committee

The association may have a nomination committee. The nomination committee's task is to propose candidates for the elections at the annual meeting. All members of the association can be elected to the nomination committee. If no nomination committee is elected, the board is responsible for its tasks.

§7 Annual meeting

The annual meeting shall be held no later than 31 March each year. For the meeting to be valid, the association's members must be notified personally at least two weeks in advance. Members shall be told the time, place and what will be discussed at the meeting. If all members of the association agree, the meeting can be valid even if the invitation arrived later than two weeks before the meeting.

The following shall always be addressed at an ordinary annual meeting:

  1. opening of the meeting
  2. decision on the validity of the meeting
  3. election of the chair of the meeting
  4. election of the secretary of the meeting
  5. election of at least one person to review the minutes after the meeting
  6. the board's report on last year's activities
  7. the board's report on last year's finances
  8. the auditors' report on the board's work last year
  9. decision on discharge from liability for last year's board
  10. proposals from the board and members
  11. decision on this year's activity plan
  12. decision on this year's budget and any membership fee
  13. election of this year's board
  14. election of this year's auditor
  15. election of this year's nomination committee
  16. closing of the meeting

If the board, the auditor or at least one quarter of the association's members so wish, the association shall hold an extraordinary annual meeting. It can be held at any time of the year. The same validity requirements apply to an extraordinary annual meeting as to an ordinary one. At an extraordinary annual meeting, only what is stated in the invitation can be addressed.

§8 Voting

All members present at the annual meeting have one vote each. In votes, the proposal that receives the most votes wins. If a vote is tied twice in a row, the proposal is considered rejected. If the vote concerns an election and the result is tied twice in a row, it is decided by lot. Votes that are neither for nor against a proposal are not counted.

All board members present at a board meeting have the right to vote. In votes, the proposal that receives the most votes wins. If a vote is tied twice in a row, the proposal is considered rejected. Votes that are neither for nor against a proposal are not counted.

§9 Amending the bylaws

The association's bylaws can only be amended at an annual meeting, if the proposed amendment is included in the invitation to the meeting. For the amendment to take effect, at least twice as many must vote for the amendment as against it.

§10 Dissolution

The annual meeting can decide that the association shall be dissolved. The association is not dissolved as long as there are at least three members who want to run it. If the association is dissolved, all its debts shall be paid and its accounts closed. If the association has money or property left, these shall be donated to a non-profit association with a similar purpose or returned to Sverok.
